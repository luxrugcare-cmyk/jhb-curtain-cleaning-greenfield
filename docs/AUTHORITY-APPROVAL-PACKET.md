# Authority Approval Packet — JCCI, FEDHASA Inland, SAPOA

Date: 2026-08-14
Status: internal decision packet only. No outreach, applications, payments or listings have been submitted.

## Objective

Prepare the exact information needed to decide whether JHB Curtain Cleaning should pursue legitimate business-association memberships that could support commercial pipeline development and, secondarily, trustworthy local authority signals.

This packet does not authorize contacting, applying to, paying, or listing with any organisation.

## Decision principles

Approve only where the commercial/networking value is defensible without relying on SEO benefit.

Do not join primarily for a backlink. Do not buy links. Do not submit bulk directory listings. Any website link or directory inclusion must be an incidental benefit of a legitimate membership relationship.

## 1. FEDHASA Inland

### Current public evidence

- Gauteng falls under FEDHASA Inland.
- Supplier businesses can join as Trusted Partners.
- Published Trusted Partner annual fees start at R2,077 for 1–5 staff and increase by staff band.
- FEDHASA exposes member-directory and marketplace functions.
- Hospitality-sector relevance is direct for hotels, lodges, venues and related commercial buyers.

### Missing facts to confirm before approval

1. JHB Curtain Cleaning's correct staff-count band.
2. Whether directory inclusion is automatic for Trusted Partners.
3. Whether marketplace inclusion is automatic or separately paid.
4. Whether the member profile includes a website link.
5. Whether there is an application fee, joining fee or other once-off charge.
6. Renewal terms and cancellation/notice period.
7. Whether supplier categories include cleaning/fabric-care services.
8. Whether Inland networking events are accessible to Trusted Partners under the base fee.

### Approval gate

Recommend APPROVE FOR CONTACT if the business intends to target hotels/hospitality and the correct staff-band fee is commercially acceptable.

Do not approve payment until the answers above are documented.

## 2. JCCI

### Current public evidence

- Johannesburg business-association relevance is strong.
- Public member benefits include business contacts, B2B advertising, training and advocacy.
- JCCI publicly states that a member-directory listing can link to the member's own website for R61 incl. VAT.
- The current base membership subscription amount is not publicly exposed on the pages checked.

### Missing facts to confirm before approval

1. Current annual membership subscription for a business of JHB Curtain Cleaning's size/type.
2. Once-off joining/application fee, if any.
3. Renewal date mechanics and cancellation terms.
4. Whether directory listing is included in base membership.
5. Whether the R61 website-link charge is once-off or recurring.
6. Whether the listing permits business description, service categories and contact details.
7. Which networking/B2B events are included versus separately ticketed.
8. Whether there are member-to-member procurement or referral opportunities relevant to cleaning/facilities services.

### Approval gate

Recommend APPROVE FOR CONTACT if Johannesburg B2B networking and procurement exposure are strategically useful independently of the directory link.

Do not approve membership solely to obtain the R61 website link.

## 3. SAPOA

### Current public evidence

- SAPOA serves the commercial-property sector.
- The public membership directory includes a Cleaning Services category.
- Public member listings can include website fields.
- Member benefits include industry networking, access to sector leaders, research, education and committee participation.
- Public pages checked do not expose a current supplier/cleaning-services tariff.

### Missing facts to confirm before approval

1. Correct membership class for a cleaning/fabric-care supplier.
2. Current annual membership fee for that class.
3. Any application/joining fee.
4. Whether Cleaning Services directory inclusion is automatic.
5. Whether the directory profile includes a live website link.
6. Whether supplier members can attend property-owner/property-manager networking events.
7. Whether committee participation is available at the applicable membership tier.
8. Renewal/cancellation terms.

### Approval gate

Recommend APPROVE FOR CONTACT if commercial-property managers, landlords and facilities decision-makers are a target pipeline and the supplier-tier fee is proportionate to expected business value.

Do not approve payment before membership class and fee are confirmed.
