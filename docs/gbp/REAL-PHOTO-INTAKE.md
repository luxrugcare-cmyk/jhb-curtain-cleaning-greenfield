## Capture guidance

- Cover: landscape 16:9 or 4:3, real curtains, real room, natural light, and a real technician only with permission.
- Operational: 1200x900 minimum preferred, clean equipment, professional PPE/uniform, no customer personal data, and no property details unless approved.
- Before/after: use the same viewpoint and similar lighting; do not exaggerate with heavy edits.
- Approval must be destination-specific: GBP, website, and social are separate approvals.
- Never upload generated technicians, team, vehicles, customer/property scenes, hotel/office jobs, cleaning-process scenes, or before/after imagery as real evidence.
