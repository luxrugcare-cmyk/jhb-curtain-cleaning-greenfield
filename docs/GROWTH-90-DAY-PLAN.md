## 2. Competitor pattern worth emulating

Current South African organic results show two useful models:

1. Specialist authority: blindandcurtaincleaners.co.za ranks strongly for curtain-cleaning terms.
2. Service × location architecture: Cleanlab ranks a large set of carpet, upholstery and other cleaning queries through dedicated city/service pages.

Chem-Dry also demonstrates the value of broad service authority and Gauteng location coverage.

JHB Curtain Cleaning should combine these models without becoming a generic cleaning directory:

- retain curtain cleaning as the brand-defining specialist service;
- build strong adjacent textile-service pages;
- strengthen the six existing area pages first;
- expand geography only where search, lead or Search Console data supports it;
- preserve separate residential and commercial conversion paths.

## 5. Commercial-sector programme

Commercial SEO should target operational problems rather than generic cleaning terms.

Priority order:

1. Hotels & hospitality — room downtime, guest disruption, public areas, scheduled maintenance.
2. Property & facilities management — portfolio scheduling, repeatable scope, access coordination.
3. Offices & corporate — after-hours access, minimal disruption, boardrooms and common areas.
4. Venues & theatres — stage drapes, large-format curtains, public-space scheduling.
5. Healthcare — controlled access, documented scope, sensitive environments.
6. Education — holiday scheduling, halls, theatres and classrooms.

Commercial content must route to `/commercial-assessment`, never dilute the residential quote funnel.

## 7. Conversion strategy

Every organic landing page should present a single dominant next action suited to intent:

- Residential service/area pages: `Get an assessment` -> `/quote`
- Commercial pages: `Request a site assessment` -> `/commercial-assessment`
- Mobile: preserve click-to-call and WhatsApp convenience without obstructing forms.

Track at minimum:

- quote_start
- commercial_start
- residential lead submitted
- commercial lead submitted
- click-to-call
- WhatsApp click
- source / medium / campaign / landing page

Conversion optimization should begin only after enough real sessions exist to avoid reacting to noise.

## 9. Paid acquisition guardrail

Do not scale paid search until GA4 and lead attribution are proven account-side.

First paid-search test, when ready:

- exact/phrase: curtain cleaning, curtain cleaning near me, curtain cleaners;
- local: curtain cleaning Johannesburg;
- high-value adjacent: mattress cleaning near me, couch cleaning near me;
- exclude irrelevant DIY, machine rental, jobs and product-shopping traffic.

Use separate residential and commercial campaigns/landing pages.

## 10. 90-day success metrics

Baseline must be captured from Search Console and GA4 once account access is available.

Track weekly:

- indexed pages;
- non-brand organic impressions;
- non-brand clicks;
- top-10 and top-20 keyword count;
- local queries by service and area;
- organic residential leads;
- organic commercial leads;
- landing-page conversion rate;
- call / WhatsApp interactions;
- GBP calls, website clicks and direction actions if available;
- lead quality / booked-assessment rate.

Decision rule: traffic growth alone is not success. Prioritize qualified assessments and booked work.
