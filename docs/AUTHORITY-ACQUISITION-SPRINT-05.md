# Authority Acquisition Plan — Sprint 05

Date: 2026-08-14

## Objective

Increase legitimate referring-domain authority for JHB Curtain Cleaning without copying low-quality directory and URL-network patterns found in competitor backlink profiles.

## Current external-link baseline

Ubersuggest measured:
- jhbcurtaincleaning.co.za: Domain Authority 1, 0 backlinks, 0 referring domains.
- blindandcurtaincleaners.co.za: Domain Authority 5, 33 backlinks, 23 referring domains.
- cleanlabsa.co.za: Domain Authority 25, 1,629 backlinks, 202 referring domains.

Semrush backlink-matrix review showed that a material share of the competitor inventory consists of unrelated or very low-authority directories, URL-shortener/network domains and generic profile sites. These should not be copied simply because they appear in a competitor profile.

## Priority acquisition classes

### 1. Facilities-management ecosystem

Target legitimate South African facilities-management membership, supplier and service-provider directories where the business genuinely qualifies. SAFMA is a priority research target because it represents the facilities-management industry and provides member/service-directory exposure.

Best destination pages:
- /commercial
- /commercial-assessment
- /commercial/property-facilities
- relevant service page when directory rules allow deep links

### 2. Property-management ecosystem

Target credible property-industry associations and supplier/service categories where cleaning and facilities services are accepted. SAPOA is a priority research target because its membership ecosystem includes service-provider categories relevant to commercial property.

Best destination pages:
- /commercial/property-facilities
- /commercial
- /services/curtain-cleaning

### 3. Hospitality ecosystem

Target hospitality supplier directories, member marketplaces and editorial/resource opportunities. FEDHASA is a priority research target because its membership and supplier ecosystem is directly aligned with hotels and hospitality properties.

Best destination pages:
- /commercial/hotels-hospitality
- /commercial-assessment
- /services/curtain-cleaning

### 4. Johannesburg business citations

Prioritise established Johannesburg/Gauteng business organisations, local chambers and high-quality business directories only where listings are moderated, business details are verifiable and the platform is not primarily an SEO link farm.

Use consistent NAP data and point the primary website field to the canonical www domain.

### 5. Supplier / manufacturer / trade relationships

Ask genuine textile, curtain, blind, interior, property and hospitality partners whether they maintain approved installer, service-partner, supplier or resource pages. Do not exchange money solely for followed links and do not manufacture reciprocal-link networks.

### 6. Editorial and resource links

Use the existing advice cluster as linkable assets:
- /advice/how-on-site-curtain-cleaning-works
- /advice/curtain-cleaning-prices
- /advice/can-curtains-be-cleaned-without-taking-them-down
- /advice/carpet-cleaning-guide
- /advice/rug-and-persian-rug-cleaning-guide

Pitch these only when they genuinely answer an editor's or partner's resource need.

## Rejection criteria

Do not pursue a source when most of the following are true:
- unrelated to South Africa, property, cleaning, interiors, hospitality or facilities;
- automatically generated pages with no editorial review;
- URL-shortener, bookmark-network or bulk-submit footprint;
- pages overloaded with unrelated outbound links;
- explicit sale of followed links or guaranteed ranking claims;
- no visible audience or business purpose beyond SEO;
- duplicated network domains with near-identical templates.

## Outreach evidence standard

For every target, record:
1. organisation/domain;
2. relevance category;
3. qualifying membership/listing requirement;
4. proposed destination page;
5. whether payment is for legitimate membership/service rather than link placement;
6. status and date;
7. live link URL once acquired;
8. follow/nofollow status only as a measurement, not as the sole quality criterion.

## Measurement

Review referring domains monthly. The goal is not raw backlink count; it is a growing set of relevant, legitimate referring domains that support the commercial and local service architecture.

Do not remove or disavow competitor-style low-quality links unless they actually point to JHB Curtain Cleaning and there is evidence they create a material risk. Avoid creating them in the first place.
