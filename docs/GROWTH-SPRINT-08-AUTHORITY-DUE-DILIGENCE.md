## Authority due diligence — verified 2026-08-14

### 1. FEDHASA Inland — strongest currently quantified candidate

Official public evidence confirms that Gauteng businesses fall under FEDHASA Inland and that supplier businesses can apply as `Trusted Partners`.

Published Trusted Partner fees:

| Staff band | Annual | Monthly debit order |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1–5 staff | R2,077 | R197 |
| 6–15 staff | R3,280 | R273 |
| 16–35 staff | R4,232 | R386 |
| 36–55 staff | R5,760 | R480 |
| 56–100 staff | R8,082 | R729 |
| 101–150 staff | R12,570 | R1,137 |
| 151+ staff | R16,415 | R1,485 |

Public FEDHASA navigation exposes both a members directory and marketplace. This makes the organisation relevant for JHB Curtain Cleaning's hotel and hospitality pipeline independently of SEO value.

Decision: **STRONG CANDIDATE — HOLD FOR APPROVAL.** Confirm JHB Curtain Cleaning's current staff band and exact Trusted Partner directory/marketplace inclusion terms before any application or payment.

Official sources:
- https://fedhasa.co.za/membership-fees/
- https://fedhasa.co.za/join-fedhasa-inland-today/
- https://fedhasa.co.za/members-directory/

### 2. JCCI — strong Johannesburg business-development fit; subscription amount still unresolved

JCCI publicly describes itself as a subscription-based Johannesburg business association. Published member benefits include business contacts, B2B advertising, training, advocacy and local/international business links.

JCCI's published B2B marketing page states that a link from a member-directory listing to a member's own website costs **R61 incl. VAT**. This link is not, by itself, a reason to join; the commercial value must come from Johannesburg business-development relevance.

The current public pages checked on 2026-08-14 do **not** expose the base membership subscription amount or renewal terms.

Decision: **INVESTIGATE / LIKELY APPLY IF COMMERCIAL TERMS FIT.** Obtain the current membership fee, joining terms, renewal terms and directory inclusion details before approval. No contact has been made in this sprint.

Official sources:
- https://jcci.co.za/memberships/
- https://www.jcci.co.za/b2b-marketing/
- https://www.jcci.co.za/

### 3. SAPOA — strong commercial-property audience; membership price not public

SAPOA's public directory includes a `Cleaning Services` category, and public member listings can include company website fields. SAPOA also states that membership provides networking platforms, access to industry leaders, education, research and participation in property-industry committees.

The public pages checked on 2026-08-14 do **not** provide a current general membership fee or a clearly defined supplier/cleaning-services membership tariff.

Decision: **INVESTIGATE — HOLD FOR APPROVAL.** Confirm the eligible membership class for a cleaning/fabric-care supplier, current annual fee, directory inclusion, website-link treatment and renewal terms before any application.

Official sources:
- https://sapoa.org.za/directory/
- https://sapoa.org.za/membership-directory/
- https://sapoa.org.za/why-become-a-member/
