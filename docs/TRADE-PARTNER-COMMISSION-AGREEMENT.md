## 1. Executive Summary

As a curtain manufacturer or interior decorator, your clients constantly ask: *"How do I clean and maintain these custom curtains without ruining them?"*

Traditional off-site dry cleaners create massive liabilities:
- High risk of fabric shrinkage (especially on linen, silk interlinings, and viscose blends).
- Unhooking and rehanging 5m+ double-volume drops risks motorized track and bracket damage.
- Crushed velvet pile and peeling acrylic blackout backings.

**JHB Curtain Cleaning** solves this by cleaning bespoke curtains **on-site while hanging on their tracks**. 

Through our **10% Trade Partner Program**, you protect your craftsmanship, eliminate client complaints, and earn a **10% recurring commission** on every paid invoice.
