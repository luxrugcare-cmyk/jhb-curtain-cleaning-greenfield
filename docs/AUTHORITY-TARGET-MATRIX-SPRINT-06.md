# Authority Target Matrix — Sprint 06

Date: 2026-08-14
Status: research and prioritisation only — no memberships, payments, listings or outreach submitted.

## Objective

Convert the Sprint 05 authority strategy into a practical acquisition queue focused on legitimate South African facilities, property, hospitality and Johannesburg business ecosystems. The decision criterion is commercial relevance first; a website link is a secondary benefit.

## Scoring model

Each target is scored from 1–5 on:
- Audience fit: proximity to buyers for commercial curtain/textile cleaning.
- Commercial value: realistic opportunity for relationships, enquiries, procurement visibility or supplier discovery.
- Authority value: legitimacy and relevance of the organisation and any public business/member listing.
- Cost efficiency: 5 = low/no cost or clearly efficient; 1 = high cost relative to current evidence.
- Execution readiness: how clearly the current public information explains the route to participate.

A paid membership is recommended only when the membership itself has credible business value. Never pay solely for a followed backlink.

## Hard rejection rules

Skip any opportunity whose primary offer is:
- bulk/guaranteed backlinks;
- automated directory submission;
- paid followed links sold mainly for rankings;
- unrelated international profiles;
- bookmark/URL-shortener networks;
- scraped or unmoderated directories with no meaningful audience;
- reciprocal-link schemes.

## Measurement

Authority acquisition is successful when it produces legitimate referring organisations, real supplier visibility, qualified commercial conversations or procurement access. Track referring domains monthly, but do not optimise for raw backlink count.
